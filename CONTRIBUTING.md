# Contributing to this open research

Welcome critical academic feedback and focused, reproducible contributions.

1. Start with [the research protocol](docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) and [prior work](docs/RELATED_WORK.md).
2. Open an Issue before proposing a new algorithm, additional baseline or major scope change.
3. When reporting a paper, provide its publisher/arXiv DOI link, date, key method, and the exact claim it challenges.
4. Separate hypotheses, evidence and speculation. All empirical claims require reproducible artifacts.
5. Contributions involving code should include deterministic commands, versions, test cases and realistic compute accounting.
6. Do not add affiliations, funders, collaborators or institutional endorsements without consent.
7. New human-participant studies require suitable ethics and privacy review.
8. Please communicate in English or Spanish; bilingual summaries are especially valuable.

We welcome **negative evidence** and corrections to novelty claims. A finding that makes the research question narrower is a useful contribution.

## Proposed issue labels (maintainer to create as needed)
literature, hypothesis, experiment-design, reproducibility, ethics, documentation, good-first-issue.
