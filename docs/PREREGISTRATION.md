## Source integrity

A GitHub timestamp is **not equivalent** to a preregistration with an independent registry. Do not retroactively amend hypotheses as if they were frozen. Keep pilot explorations clearly separate.

## Proposed reporting checklist

Publish all seeds; budgets including controller/evaluation overhead; memory/compute footprints; negative outcomes; ablations; reproducibility scripts; hardware constraints and known threats to validity. Link to registered hypothesis and every final result artifact.

## Open Science Framework

Registration via OSF or a similar accepted registry should be performed only when hypotheses, datasets and metrics are fully specified. A future DOI requires an actual archival deposit or release; never invent one.
