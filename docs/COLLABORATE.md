# Research collaboration | Convocatoria académica

## Invitation (English)

We invite researchers and students in machine learning, multi-agent reinforcement learning, game theory, distributed computing, complex systems, collective intelligence and AI governance to scrutinize a **draft, falsifiable research protocol** for compute-efficient cooperative learning.

**Question:** Can adaptive peer assistance—teaching, skill repair and selective training—produce more verified learning per unit of compute than independent, competitive or fixed-cooperation baselines under heterogeneous resource constraints?

**Useful contributions:**
- Identify prior art that invalidates or narrows the proposed novelty.
- Critique pre-registration, statistical power, benchmarks and baseline fairness.
- Suggest low-cost reproducible environments and measurement standards.
- Review equations and clarify feasibility of individual-capability retention.
- Develop open benchmark adapters only after the protocol is frozen.
- Study governance, effects on communities and potential externalities.

**What we can offer today:** a public protocol and references; no validated algorithm, paper, grant or institutional affiliation is claimed.

**How to respond:** open a GitHub Issue with references and the specific research question you want to help answer, or contribute a focused pull request. Propose affiliations only with your institution's consent. This project welcomes Latin American participation but is globally open.

## Invitación (español)

Invitamos a investigadores, docentes y estudiantes de América Latina y otras regiones a revisar críticamente una propuesta abierta sobre **aprendizaje cooperativo entre agentes de IA con restricciones estrictas de cómputo**.

La pregunta no es si cooperar «suena mejor»: queremos medir si enseñar, reparar capacidades y redistribuir recursos según necesidades puede mejorar resultados verificables sin gastar más hardware ni degradar el aprendizaje previo.

Buscamos aportes en teoría de juegos, sistemas multiagente, aprendizaje continuo, evaluación reproducible, ética y gobernanza. Se puede participar señalando antecedentes que contradicen la supuesta novedad; eso es una contribución valiosa.

**Estado actual:** protocolo de investigación, no resultados experimentales ni iniciativa institucional formal.

## Suggested research groups

Appropriate potential audiences include CS and AI laboratories; economics and game theory groups; computational social sciences; cooperative and community technology initiatives; open-science groups and public universities in Argentina and Latin America.

Do not represent any university or researcher as a collaborator until they have explicitly agreed.
